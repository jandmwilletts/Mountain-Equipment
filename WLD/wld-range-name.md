# WLD range name: **Redoubt**

**Use `Redoubt` as the collection / order name for mixed Mountain Equipment WLD kit.**

Keep `WLD` as the programme tag. Keep Mountain Equipment product names on quote lines (Citadel, Fitzroy, Exo). Do not rename those SKUs.

---

## What WLD is

WLD is Mountain Equipment’s **Work, Land & Defence** programme: bespoke or higher-spec pieces for professional users (land work, remote operations, defence), not a consumer collection like Stonewear or the Hiking range.

Known WLD product families already have names:

| Family | Typical WLD pieces | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Citadel** | Hat WLD, Mitt WLD (also the heavy synthetic belay jacket in retail) | Severe cold, fortress-level protection |
| **Fitzroy** | Pant WLD; retail Fitzroy jacket is the lighter synthetic insulator | Wet-cold insulation that keeps working when damp |
| **Exo** | Jacket WLD (Drab and similar) | Fully taped GORE-TEX INFINIUM down “bomb shelter” |

If the pieces in front of you are only one of those families, use that family name. `Redoubt` is for a **mixed WLD order**, the same way `Ranger` was used as the order header for the Ranger HQ civilian clothing job.

---

## Why Redoubt

- Same language as Citadel: a **redoubt** is a field fortification, not a parade-ground word.
- Covers Work, Land and Defence without looking tactical or American.
- One word, easy on POs and Odoo (`Redoubt Jacket`, `Redoubt order`).
- Not used on the current Mountain Equipment catalogue (Frontier, Citadel, Fitzroy, Exo, Makalu, Manaslu, K7 and the rest are already taken).
- Does not steal a mountain name ME may want later.

**Quote layout**

- Order / range header: **Redoubt**
- Programme: **WLD (Work, Land & Defence)**
- Line description: ME product name + WLD, e.g. `Citadel Hat WLD`, `Fitzroy Pant WLD`, `Exo Jacket WLD`

---

## If Redoubt is not used

| Name | Use when | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Warden** | The job is land management / countryside as much as defence | Softer; less like an ME product family |
| **Cheviot** | You want a classic ME mountain name (border hills, training country) | Sounds like an official ME line; ME may object or collide later |
| **Scarp** | You want a short, design-led name | Less obvious to a buyer on first hearing |

Avoid: Frontier (already an ME soft shell), Sentry, Vanguard, Keep, Bastion (too generic / tacticool), training-area names (Otterburn, Sennybridge).

## Do not do

- Do not print `Redoubt` on garments in place of Citadel / Fitzroy / Exo.
- Do not mix Redoubt and Ranger on one PO unless the same supplier is filling both.
- Do not treat WLD as the range name on a customer quote; it is the programme, and most buyers will not know what it means.
- Do not take CI buy or CI sell from the stock list when the master has a price. The master wins.
